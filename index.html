    Fees students can pay

    GroupFeePrice (£)PerNoteBelt levelActions

    For each class they attend, students choose per lesson (the fee with Per = “lesson” or “class”) or per month (Per = “month”). For Karate the monthly fee is the one matching their belt: Beginner (no belt – orange), Intermediate (yellow – green), Advanced (purple). Brown belt and above see “On application” for monthly. Other fees, such as membership and gradings, don’t show to students. Copy link gives a PayPal link you can paste into a message or notice. Remember to press Save.
